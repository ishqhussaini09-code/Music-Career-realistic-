## 📖 How to Play

### Objective
Reach **100 Fame** to become an ICON and win the game!

### Core Mechanics

**📱 Social Tab**
- Upload performance videos to gain subscribers and views
- Build your fanbase for passive income

**🎧 Studio Tab**
- Record songs using studio budget ($50 per song)
- Hire a producer to boost quality
- Release songs for fame and revenue

**💰 Business Tab**
- Busk on streets to earn quick cash
- Monitor monthly income and expenses
- Track your career phase progression

**❤️ Health Tab**
- Manage health, stress, and passion
- Rest on weekends to recover
- Balance work and wellness

### Stats Explained

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Vocal** | Improves song quality |
| **Songwriting** | Improves song quality |
| **Stage** | Improves busking earnings |
| **Creativity** | Improves song quality |
| **Health** | Health < 0 = Game Over |
| **Stress** | Damages health over time |
| **Passion** | Motivation & engagement |

---

## 🎯 Tips to Win

1. **Record multiple songs** - Release them when you have enough fame
2. **Hire a producer** - Boosts song quality significantly
3. **Balance activities** - Don't neglect health and stress
4. **Busk regularly** - Easy way to earn money early game
5. **Upload videos** - Builds subscriber base for passive income
6. **Rest when needed** - Recovery is crucial for long-term success
